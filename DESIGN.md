# Approved visual direction
Style 2 / Knuffelwereld from the approved character sheets. Cream, soft peach, lilac and sage; dark plum text. Tactile plush characters carry the experience, with readable native controls and 48dp minimum actions. Large character stage, short Dutch instructions, four destinations: Thuis, Leren, Dieren, Winkel. Portrait and landscape supported through native scroll layout; tablet content capped in width. Audio is optional; every spoken instruction is also visible.

Animation: separate idle/walk/talk/sleep frames for the entire cast, gentle motion and care props; action feedback is temporary. Respect Android animation-scale setting. Frame poses are blended with small procedural movement; these are 2D assets, not rigged 3D models.
